Delete your GraffitiTap account

In the app

Open GraffitiTap, tap the profile icon at the top, scroll to the bottom and choose Delete my account.

Without the app

Write to graffititap@protonmail.com from the email address of your Google account, asking for deletion.

What gets deleted

Your account, posted pieces with their photos, comments and hearts are permanently deleted. We keep no copies, except as required by law. Email requests are handled within 30 days.
