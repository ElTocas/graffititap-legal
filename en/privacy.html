Privacy

This notice explains what data GraffitiTap uses and why. Data controller: Tok. Contact for any request: graffititap@protonmail.com.

What data we collect

Account: if you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and an identifier for your account. Your name and email stay private: other people only see your nickname, which you can change in your profile. If you use the app as a guest and leave a heart, comment or report, we create an anonymous identifier linked to that phone.

Content: the photos, titles, descriptions, artists, tags and comments you post, and the nickname or name you choose for comments. They are public: anyone using the app can see them. The hearts you leave and your icon are also linked to your identifier.

Location: the app reads your location only while you use it, to show nearby pieces, distance filters and routes, and to place a piece you post. We save only the location of posted pieces, which is public. We don't save your movements.

Photos: before sending them, the app resizes them and removes the file's hidden data, including GPS position. The checks for explicit content and for the subject of the photo happen on your phone: the photo isn't sent to anyone for these checks. The subject model is Google's ML Kit, which works on the phone but may contact Google for technical updates.

Reports and blocks: who you report or block, and why. These aren't visible to other users.

The phone keeps your theme, language and music preferences, and your acceptance of the terms. The app has no advertising or profiling and doesn't sell data.

Who the data goes to

Supabase stores accounts, content and photos. Google handles Google sign-in. Maps come from OpenStreetMap and addresses from Nominatim, also by OpenStreetMap, which receive your IP address and the area viewed or searched. If you open directions you move to Google Maps. If you search Wikimedia Commons, Wikimedia receives the area searched. Some of these providers may process data outside the European Union, with the safeguards required by law.

Why we use it

To let you use the service you asked for, under the Terms of use, and for our legitimate interest in keeping the community safe and protecting the service from abuse.

How long

We keep your data for as long as you have the account. Your pieces and comments stay until you delete them.

Deleting your data

In the app: Profile, then "Delete my account". Your account, pieces, photos, comments and hearts are deleted immediately. You can also request deletion without the app by writing to graffititap@protonmail.com. Guest data is linked to the phone: to remove it, write to us and indicate the comment concerned.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, limit its use, receive it in a readable format, and object to its processing, by writing to graffititap@protonmail.com. You can also contact the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

The app isn't intended for anyone under 14.
