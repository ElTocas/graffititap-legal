Terms of use

By using GraffitiTap you accept these terms. They are written to be understood: if something isn't clear, write to us at graffititap@protonmail.com.

What GraffitiTap is

GraffitiTap is a shared map of street art. You can look at it without an account. Posting a piece requires a Google account. As a guest you can leave hearts and comments, and report content. You must be at least 14 to use the app.

Community rules

Not allowed: fascist, racist, sexist, homophobic or hateful content in any form; threats, harassment and insults towards other people; sexual or violent content; photos showing recognizable people without their consent; other people's personal data, such as home addresses, phone numbers or the identity of someone who made a work if it isn't already public; spam and content unrelated to street art.

We may remove this content and block the accounts that post it, even without notice.

Your content

The photos and text you post remain yours. By posting them you give us permission, free of charge and for as long as they stay online, to store them and show them to other people in the app. Only post photos you took yourself or have the right to use. The works you photograph belong to their authors: if you name an artist, do it correctly, and don't name them if they don't want to be identified.

You can edit or delete your pieces at any time, and delete your account from your profile page.

Reports and blocks

You can report any piece or comment from the three-dot menu, and block whoever posted it. Content reported by three different people is hidden until we review it. We review reports as soon as possible. If a work concerns you and you want it removed, write to us.

Take care

GraffitiTap doesn't encourage making works without permission or entering forbidden or dangerous places: when you visit a wall, stay in accessible places and respect the law and your own safety. Locations, routes and content are added by users and may be inaccurate: routes don't account for closed roads or hazards.
